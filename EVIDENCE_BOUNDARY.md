# Evidence Boundary

## What timestamped records can support

A timestamped record may support:

- **prior existence** — an idea, hypothesis, observation, or framework element existed by a certain date;
- **provenance** — the work can be traced to a particular development sequence;
- **time-to-insight evidence** — a research process identified a practical issue before later market discussion or product convergence;
- **research continuity** — later work can be linked to earlier hypotheses and revisions.

## What timestamped records do **not** prove by themselves

They do not establish:

- causation;
- copying or misappropriation;
- influence on a later company or product;
- exclusive ownership of a broad idea;
- patentability or enforceable IP rights;
- product-market fit;
- enterprise validation;
- commercial success;
- model consciousness, personhood, subjective emotion, or legal autonomy.

## Market convergence is not validation

When a later product, company announcement, or industry practice moves in a similar direction, this repository may record it as **market-convergence evidence**.

Market convergence can increase the relevance of a prior hypothesis. It does not prove that the earlier work caused the later development, that the later party knew of the work, or that the hypothesis has been validated in operation.

## Research claim firewall

My Champion Labs uses three practical claim levels:

- **Established / directly observed:** what the record itself demonstrates.
- **Candidate / inferential:** a plausible interpretation or hypothesis that still requires testing.
- **Speculative / prohibited as fact:** claims such as consciousness, legal personhood, full autonomy, or causal influence without evidence.

The purpose of this boundary is not to weaken the research. It is to keep the evidence interpretable.
