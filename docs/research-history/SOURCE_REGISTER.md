## Editorial decisions

- G1 is a new, owner-authorized GitHub reading selection, not a replacement or full reproduction of either supplied edition.
- Selected paragraphs retain the Word source wording, including historical counts and uncertainty. Each group is labelled and omissions are disclosed. Chapter headings and explanatory notes are editorial additions.
- Earlier credentialing, self-funding, and autonomy proposals are not promoted into current EIP commitments. The current framework and historical record are explicitly separated.
- Omitted private-repository screenshot transcriptions, private evidence references in the original appendices, third-party comment bodies, source images, implementation details, and the statistical or rhetorical portions of unit 51.
- The source account of author-led concepts and AI assistance is attributed, not independently certified. No absence-of-AI-authorship claim is inferred from multiple posts or hashes.
- This is a targeted selection and public-boundary review, not an independent fact-check of all 52 units or permission to publish the full source collection.
