# Public Research Scope

My Champion Labs selectively explores questions at the boundary of AI systems and real organizational work.

Current public research themes include:

- human–AI operating models;
- Human-in-the-Loop cognitive function and review burden;
- organizational learning vs model learning;
- reusable enterprise capability;
- agent identity, delegation, authority, and bounded execution;
- longitudinal trajectories and scaffold dependence;
- agent formation and progressive withdrawal of supervisory cognition;
- naturalistic interaction and compressed coordination cues;
- AI individualization as a trajectory-level hypothesis;
- cross-model and multi-agent collaboration architecture;
- research provenance and evidence boundaries;
- generative systems as recursive projective environments for human relational self-observation.

The initial public note for the final theme is available in
[`generative-relational-psychology/`](../generative-relational-psychology/).

## Important terminology boundary

Terms such as **trajectory**, **individualization**, **formation**, **relational texture**, and **skill-like coordination structure** are used behaviorally and operationally.

They do not imply AI consciousness, human-like subjective attachment, legal personhood, or independent moral agency.

## Publication discipline

Public material is selected for provenance and research communication.

Private source conversations, proprietary implementation logic, detailed EIP™ methods, confidential third-party information, and unapproved candidate material are not automatically part of the public record.
