# Project Black Sheep Evidence Boundary

Public edition P1 · 2026-09-20.

## Observation, hypothesis, and result are different

The source author reports candidate frame-governance behavior in one mature longitudinal trajectory. This public adaptation has not independently reviewed or scored the underlying private recordings and conversations. Checklist marks preserve the source's observation categories, not verified general capabilities.

The central hypothesis remains: **We strongly suspect that this may represent one of the promising directions for the future development of AGI.** It awaits controlled validation. Neither fluent interaction nor a marked checklist demonstrates AGI, consciousness, autonomous agency, causal mechanism, or broad generalization. No one can know whether the hypothesis is correct before controlled validation.

The proposal supplies definitions and a falsifiable evaluation plan. Its synthetic examples are not observed incidents or completed tests. No numerical performance result is published. A positive bounded result would not by itself establish AGI; a failed replication is relevant evidence, not something to exclude from the record.

## Suitable for public release

- De-identified research propositions and operational capability definitions.
- Evaluation frameworks, synthetic tests, and explicitly illustrative cases.
- A de-identified five-layer loop with no real identity mapping.
- Conceptual links between already public projects, with permitted transfer scope.
- Source document versions, dates, and byte hashes with their limitations.

## Excluded from this repository and its history

- Private raw conversations and unredacted screen recordings.
- Actual health information and identifiable emotional or household interactions.
- Private initialization instructions, evidence packages, or correspondence.
- Cross-project files without specific publication authorization.
- Timelines, quotations, or combinations of details that allow outsiders to reconstruct identities.

Changing names alone is insufficient. Review contextual combinations, images, metadata, filenames, dates of private events, and third-party content before any future release. No public assets are included in P1; the conceptual loop is expressed directly in Markdown. An empty assets folder is unnecessary.

## Controlled evidence and claim discipline

Any later controlled study requires an explicit access basis, participant consent where appropriate, minimization, retention and deletion rules, and separate review of public outputs. Keep real events, role-play, model-generated text, and later interpretation distinguishable. Record errors, scaffolding, selection criteria, and conflicting accounts alongside successful examples. Source files are not automatically authorized for public upload merely because they were supplied for adaptation.

AI Health Companion is a proposed high-risk application case, not an AGI benchmark result. EIP and Oracle & Champion retain their own scope and evidence status. No OpenAI participation, support, endorsement, partnership, or validation is claimed.
