# AI Health Companion

People do not live in forms. Details that matter to care arrive through meals, changed routines, passing complaints, and stories—not only through scheduled questionnaires. Participants and supporters often have to reconstruct those fragments before another person can use them.

**AI Health Companion is not another health-education chatbot. It is a relationship-aware, consent-governed translation and coordination layer between everyday life and formal care.**

The proposed workflow turns natural conversation into candidate observations, asks the participant to confirm or reject them, and prepares minimal, authorized summaries with sources, gaps, and uncertainty visible. The first outputs to test are a three-day summary and a visit-ready brief. The value hypothesis is less repeated recording and explanation—not more surveillance.

Start with the [Main Proposal V3](docs/main-proposal/MAIN_PROPOSAL_V3.md) or its [Traditional Chinese edition](zh-TW/MAIN_PROPOSAL_V3.md). Within the limited market scan conducted to date, we did not identify a single product publicly demonstrating this complete combination. ## Project boundaries

Safety remains a product responsibility. This proposal does not establish a released clinical product, diagnosis, prescribing, independent medication adjustment, or replacement of clinicians or emergency services. See V3's dedicated safety section and the governance documents for the retained requirements.

This project is distinct from Oracle & Champion. The proposed EIP middleware is an application design direction that references EIP governance principles; it is not a claim of an implemented middleware product, an endorsed clinical deployment, or validation of EIP. The [EIP repository](https://github.com/HillaryFan/Ephemeral-Intelligence-Protocol) remains the framework of record.

Product requirements described here still need engineering, appropriate clinical review, participant consent, and testing. A persona or initialization prompt cannot establish access isolation, reliable deletion, autonomous monitoring, or emergency response.

No medication-package images, medical photographs, private reports, email screenshots, commercial evidence packages, or complete household initialization instructions are included. Private participant records and business materials must remain outside this public repository, not merely in a differently named folder.
