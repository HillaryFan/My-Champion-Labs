# AI Health Companion

An exploratory product project for a configurable AI companion, diabetes education support, and consent-based care coordination. The design asks whether natural conversation can reduce recording and coordination burden while preserving autonomy, privacy, and safe escalation to people.

**Status: V2 public, de-identified documentation edition. Not a released clinical product or validated medical system.** This project does not replace physicians, nurses, diabetes educators, emergency services, or individualized care plans. It must not diagnose, prescribe, or independently change insulin or other medication.

## Authoritative sources and language

The project is based on the V2 Main Proposal (initiated 2026-09-16; updated 2026-09-17) and V2 Addendum (2026-09-17). English is the primary repository reading language. Separate [Traditional Chinese public editions](zh-TW/README.md) preserve the same public scope. Neither public edition supersedes the controlled source documents. Translation and public packaging do not create a new source version.

The public text replaces personal identities, family relationships, and individual medical histories with role-level scenarios. It is an edited public adaptation, not an unredacted translation. No participant outcomes or clinical effectiveness are claimed.

## Project boundaries

This project is distinct from Oracle & Champion. The proposed EIP middleware is an application design direction that references EIP governance principles; it is not a claim of an implemented middleware product, an endorsed clinical deployment, or validation of EIP. The [EIP repository](https://github.com/HillaryFan/Ephemeral-Intelligence-Protocol) remains the framework of record.

Product requirements described here still need engineering, appropriate clinical review, participant consent, and testing. A persona or initialization prompt cannot establish access isolation, reliable deletion, autonomous monitoring, or emergency response.

No medication-package images, medical photographs, private reports, email screenshots, commercial evidence packages, or complete household initialization instructions are included. Private participant records and business materials must remain outside this public repository, not merely in a differently named folder.
