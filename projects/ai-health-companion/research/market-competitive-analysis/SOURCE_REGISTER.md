## Editorial decisions

- Replaced personal names, intimate-role labels, and real household references with adult participant, older-adult participant, and authorized supporter.
- Published Markdown adaptations only. Original DOCX/PDF files, personal metadata, and private prototype materials remain unpublished.
- Replaced “complete clinical safety” scores with capability questions. Proposed My Champion Labs features are explicitly unimplemented or unvalidated requirements.
- Reframed market white space and defensibility as hypotheses, not established absence of competitors or exclusive invention.
- Distinguished contextual memory from participant-controlled retention, deletion, and sharing.
- Kept the phased product exploration, with commercial and evidence questions labeled as editorial analysis rather than measured findings.
- Preserved source links, including unverified inherited references, with their actual review status.
- Kept V2 product documents unchanged. This market scan cannot silently change approved product scope, safety rules, or publication boundaries.
