**Research date: 2026-09-17 · Public adaptation: M1 · Status: exploratory desk research.**

This English public edition adapts the supplied market scan, removes personal and household references, and adds a focused check of official product descriptions. It is supporting research, not a new version of the [V2 Main Proposal](../../docs/main-proposal/MAIN_PROPOSAL_V2.md) or [V2 Addendum](../../docs/addenda/ADDENDUM_V2.md). Those remain the product's authoritative sources. ## Assessment

The reviewed market already contains chronic-condition coaching, conversational monitoring, companionship, caregiver communication, glucose sharing, and proxy record access. These are meaningful precedents. Their existence does not establish willingness to pay for this project's proposed combination.

The opportunity hypothesis is a **participant-controlled health companionship and care-coordination workflow**: useful daily conversation, bounded health support, selective sharing, deliberate memory retention, and traceable handoff. The reviewed pages do not establish a single exact match to all proposed requirements. This is a finding about a limited source set, not proof of an empty market, unique invention, patent novelty, or a defensible business.

My Champion Labs is a proposal and prototype plan. Its intended capabilities must not be scored as delivered features alongside marketed products. No clinical effectiveness, regulatory clearance, or operational readiness is claimed for this project.

## Method and limits

The supplied scan selected ten competitors or adjacent products. This adaptation checks their public descriptions, emphasizing first-party sources; it is not an exhaustive search, hands-on evaluation, procurement review, or clinical evidence appraisal. Product versions, regional availability, paid tiers, institutional configurations, and current commercial availability were not comprehensively established.

Vendor descriptions indicate what is publicly represented, not independently verified performance. “Not established in the reviewed sources” means a question remains open; it does not mean a feature is absent. Marketing pages cannot support a rating of “complete clinical safety.” Regulatory claims require product-specific indications and jurisdiction checks before any operational reliance.

## Capability questions, without inflated scores

The source's “complete / partial / unseen” matrix is replaced by questions that can be tested. No vendor or proposal receives a universal safety score.

| Dimension | What the scan establishes | What this project must demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relational interaction | Companionship and personalization have precedents. | Appropriate interaction improves reporting usefulness without pressure to disclose or dependence on the persona. |
| Condition support and safety | Condition-specific coaching and monitoring exist; their intended uses differ. | Clinically reviewed boundaries, reliable escalation, and measured failure handling in a bounded use case. |
| Data capture | Logs, photographs, and connected measurements appear across products. | Source attribution, correction, uncertainty, and missing-data handling; no inference that a photo is a verified measurement. |
| Supporter summaries | Reports, alerts, and record access are established adjacent functions. | Participant review and recipient-specific minimum disclosure that actually reduces coordination work. |
| Permission controls | Invitations and organizational proxy policies are precedents. | Tested scope, revocation, recipient identity, and behavior after permissions change. |
| Memory governance | Contextual memory is already marketed. | Separation of retention purposes; enforceable deletion, explicit consent, and intelligible user controls. |
| Proactivity and handoff | Scheduled contact and alerts have precedents. | Appropriate timing, acknowledged delivery, traceable sources, and clear ownership of unresolved items. |

## Six differentiation hypotheses

1. **Interaction that yields to safety.** A configurable conversational style may encourage reporting, but concerning information must trigger a clear, conservative workflow. Persona behavior must never override approved boundaries.
2. **Participant-controlled coordination.** An adult participant chooses authorized supporters, time windows, and summary scope. Supporter status must not confer access to all conversation history.
3. **Memory separated by purpose.** Health records follow an explicit, appropriate retention policy; casual conversation is temporary by default. Any retained social tags require consent and a defined purpose. Neither a prompt nor this document proves deletion is implemented.
4. **Reports based on need.** A three-day summary is an initial usability experiment. Event-triggered reporting remains a requirement to validate, including false alerts, missed events, and the availability of a responsible human recipient.
5. **Traceable handoff.** Reports distinguish observations, interpretations, missing information, applicable rule versions, and unresolved follow-up. Recipients need a way to challenge or correct the summary.
6. **Adaptable interaction across age groups.** Adult and older-adult modes may share infrastructure, but usability, consent, and safety must be evaluated separately. Success in one group does not establish transfer to another.

These are design hypotheses, not proven advantages. The source's relationship-depth versus clinical-depth diagram is treated as a qualitative framing only; there is no measured market map supporting an unoccupied quadrant.

## Positioning and staged evaluation

Proposed positioning: **a participant-controlled family health companion and care-coordination system**, with adult diabetes-related daily recording as an initial bounded scenario. This is a research positioning hypothesis; changing the label does not determine clinical or regulatory obligations.

| Stage | Bounded question | Evidence and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Adult prototype | Can conversation and a reviewed three-day brief reduce recording and coordination burden? | Begin with synthetic safety tests, then an appropriately reviewed, consented prototype. Compare completeness, correction rate, and participant/supporter time against the existing workflow. No autonomous medication changes. |
| 2. Sharing and reports | Can useful information reach the right person without excess disclosure? | Test review, recipient scope, revocation, provenance, and visit-summary usefulness. Measure unauthorized disclosure and unresolved follow-up, not just successful delivery. |
| 3. Older-adult voice co-design | Does voice reduce effort while preserving autonomy? | Small, separately consented usability work; test interruptions, return to unfinished questions, temporary conversation, and consent comprehension. |
| 4. Device or institutional integration | Does integration add enough value to justify its burden? | Evaluate only after workflow value is shown; assess interface access, reliability, staffing, clinical responsibilities, and cost. |

Engagement, data completeness, safety performance, and health outcomes are distinct endpoints. Retention or frequent conversation does not establish improved health. Prototype success should be judged against a predeclared comparator and criteria, not testimonials alone.

## Commercial questions still unanswered

- Who uses, who benefits, who pays, and who takes responsibility when a report flags a concern?
- Is a participant-reviewed brief materially better than current logs, messages, and existing reporting tools?
- Does the value survive the cost of human review, corrections, support, and escalation coverage?
- Will users tolerate the necessary consent and verification steps? Can sharing be declined without losing companionship?
- Can a partner integrate or reproduce the workflow more cheaply? What specific implementation or evidence would sustain an advantage?

The next useful proof is a small, safe comparison demonstrating better coordination with less burden and no unacceptable disclosure. Adding more personas or integrations before that result would widen the scope without resolving demand.
