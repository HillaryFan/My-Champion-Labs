# Oracle & Champion — Bounded Paid Field Probe

## Purpose

Oracle & Champion is a small real-world experiment designed to test whether a formed human–AI capability unit can produce useful, case-specific insight outside its originating research context.

It is **not** designed to prove that the model can support a full consulting business, and its primary value is not the US$500 fee itself.

The field probe asks a narrower question:

> **Will an external person with a real problem pay for, use, and act on a bounded blind-spot review produced through this capability?**

## Entry offer

### One Case. One Blind-Spot Review. One Decision Brief.

A bounded case may receive:

1. **Blind Spot** — an assumption, missing variable, or hidden dependency constraining the case.
2. **Strategic Option(s)** — plausible alternative paths.
3. **Operating Boundary** — what would need to be true for an option to be viable.
4. **Implications / Next Test** — what to test, decide, watch, or challenge next.

## Best-fit case boundary

The pilot is intended for case-specific problems at the intersection of:

- AI capability;
- human judgment and cognitive review;
- enterprise or team operating models;
- AI agents, delegation, and capability boundaries;
- emerging AI product or workflow capability;
- organizational learning and reusable capability;
- vendor / platform co-creation and capability rights.

## Out of scope

This is not:

- general management consulting;
- general market research;
- implementation-for-hire or software development;
- legal, medical, regulatory, financial, or investment advice;
- a prediction or clairvoyance service;
- a claim that every case will produce a novel or valuable answer.

## Evidence to collect

For each real engagement, the experiment should record, at a minimized and non-confidential level:

- case category;
- buyer role / organization type;
- original decision or problem;
- identified blind spot;
- whether the buyer found the output useful;
- whether the output changed a decision, test, framing, or next step;
- whether the buyer requested deeper work;
- burden required to produce the result;
- limitations and failure modes.

## Decision logic

The experiment is not required to scale.

Evidence may support:

- **Scale** — repeatable demand and useful outcomes emerge.
- **Refine** — value exists, but positioning, scope, pricing, or delivery requires change.
- **Remain bounded** — useful for a narrow class of cases only.
- **Pause** — insufficient demand, distribution, time, or operating capacity.
- **Stop** — no meaningful value or unacceptable burden.

This decision logic intentionally mirrors the evidence-before-scale discipline used in EIP™.
