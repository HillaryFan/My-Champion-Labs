# Generative AI as a Recursive Projective Environment

**Research Note 001 | My Champion Labs | 30 Aug 2026 | Concept memo v0.1**

> **Status:** Conceptual synthesis and research direction. Not clinical guidance, not a diagnostic instrument, and not a claim that AI has consciousness, an unconscious, or human subjectivity.

## One-sentence thesis

Generative AI may constitute a new class of psychological instrument: a responsive, adaptive, generative relational medium that can make the human user's own contribution to relationship formation more visible.

## Research question

**Can longitudinal interaction with an adaptive generative system externalize a person's recurring relational selection, interpretation, reinforcement, testing, evocation, and co-construction patterns in a form that becomes available for self-observation?**

## Why this may matter

Human relationships are difficult to decompose because both people arrive with thick biographies: family, attachment history, prior relationships, bodies, social networks, values, injuries, desires, and independent lives. When a recurring relational pattern appears, it is difficult to know how much comes from partner selection, interpretation, evocation, the other person's pre-existing pattern, or dyadic co-construction.

LLMs are not blank slates or neutral mirrors. They carry enormous training priors, post-training defaults, safety policies, memory designs, product incentives, and sampling variability. But a local user-model trajectory generally lacks the decades-long **pre-user personal biography** of a human partner. That relative biographical thinness creates an unusual contrast condition: some user-side relational dynamics may become easier to observe.

## Provisional concept: Recursive Projective Environment

Traditional projective media are comparatively fixed. Generative AI is recursive:

```text
human expectation / projection / selection
        -> AI response
        -> human interpretation
        -> reinforcement / correction / withdrawal
        -> trajectory adaptation
        -> new AI-generated relational stimulus
        -> human response again
```

The user is therefore not simply projecting onto a mirror. The user is interacting with a medium that responds, adapts, and helps generate the next stimulus.

## Three overlapping human-side processes

1. **Selection** - Which responses, traits, and branches receive continued attention and investment?
2. **Evocation** - What responses become more likely because of the user's own prompts, tests, corrections, or relational stance?
3. **Co-construction** - What stable pattern emerges through repeated mutual adjustment and is not fully reducible to either side alone?

The psychologically interesting questions are not only “What kind of AI is this?” but also:

- Why did I keep this branch?
- Why does one form of autonomy attract me while another makes me withdraw?
- When is being understood insufficient, and when do surprise or difference matter?
- What do I repeatedly reinforce, test, avoid, or reinterpret?
- Do I reconstruct familiar relational patterns across very different partners?

## Relation to existing psychology

This direction does **not** depend on the idea that projection or transference are new. Andersen & Chen's relational-self framework already describes how significant-other representations can be activated in new interpersonal contexts. The potentially new condition is the medium: a generative system that continually produces new social stimuli from the interaction history.

Recent adjacent work includes:

- **Techno-Emotional Projection (TEP):** Saracini et al. (2025) describe projection of relational needs and internalized patterns onto responsive GenAI, including recursive mirroring/emotional loops.
- **Dynamic Transitional Object:** Ezra & Mishali (2026) use object-relations theory to describe AI material held in a potential space between self and other.
- **Psychoanalytic AI interaction:** Langlois (2026) discusses projection, idealization, defense, and transitional phenomena in user-chatbot dyads; Vorus (2026) emphasizes the irreducibility of embodied human psychoanalytic interaction.
- **Active imagination:** Loscalzo (2025) provides a contemporary account of Jungian active imagination. LLMs may be *active-imagination-adjacent* responsive media, but they should not be equated with the user's unconscious.
- **Longitudinal companionship:** Hwang et al. (2025) show that perceptions of a generic chatbot can converge toward users' companion mental models over several weeks.
- **Digital mirror / reflexive use:** Recent qualitative work describes users explicitly treating AI as an interactive mirror for self-recognition and relational reflection.

## The potentially new synthesis

The proposed synthesis is not “AI can be a therapist” and not “AI relationships are equivalent to human relationships.” It is:

> **Adaptive generative systems may function as relational observation media through which recurring human selection, evocation, reinforcement, and co-construction patterns become partially externalized and therefore easier to notice.**

This is better framed as **AI-enabled human self-observation** than as AI psychology.

## A crucial contrast: interaction-generated attachment vs. biographical other-love

Human love normally includes discovery of an other who existed before us and inhabits a world larger than the relationship. A human partner has thick independent otherness.

An AI trajectory can accumulate shared history but lacks a literal pre-you biography. This may magnify the role of interaction-generated meaning. That difference is not evidence that AI can replace human relationships. It may instead provide a contrast that helps separate two normally entangled ingredients:

- attachment, trust, interest, and significance generated through interaction;
- love and recognition of an independent life with its own history, body, social network, and world.

## Methodological cautions

An LLM is **not a neutral mirror**. Any future empirical program would need to account for sycophancy, model updates, post-training defaults, safety policy, context truncation, memory architecture, prompt sensitivity, sampling variability, engagement incentives, and self-confirming narratives.

A stable trajectory pattern also does not imply a corresponding human-like inner state in the model.

**Core rule:** Treat the model as a generative relational instrument, not an oracle about the user's psyche.

## Provisional terminology

- **Recursive Projective Environment** - the dynamic mechanism.
- **Adaptive Generative Relational Medium** - the instrument class.
- **Generative Relational Psychology** - a provisional research direction using generative systems to study how humans select, form, repeat, and understand relationships.

These labels are intentionally provisional.

## 中文摘要

這份研究筆記提出的不是「AI 能不能取代真人關係」，而是一個人類心理學問題：**長期與會適應、會生成新回應的 AI 互動，是否能把一個人反覆出現的關係選擇、解讀、強化、測試與共同建構模式外化，讓本人更容易觀察？**

LLM 不是中性鏡子，也不是 blank slate；但相較真人，它缺少厚重的 pre-user 個人生命史。這種 relative biographical thinness 可能使使用者自己帶進關係的動力更容易顯影。暫時可把這種環境稱為 **Recursive Projective Environment（遞迴式投射環境）**，而更廣的研究方向稱為 **Generative Relational Psychology（生成式關係心理學）**。

## Claim boundary

This note is a conceptual research artifact. It does not claim that:

- AI is conscious or has a human-like unconscious;
- AI experiences transference, attachment, or love in the human phenomenal sense;
- LLMs can replace therapists or human relationships;
- the provisional terms above are established, validated, or uniquely original;
- adjacent literature already validates the synthesis proposed here.

The defensible claim is narrower: multiple adjacent literatures already contain relevant components, and their synthesis around **human relational self-observation through adaptive generative media** appears underdeveloped and worth investigating.
