## Scope

This is a research/provenance surface, not a clinical service and not a companion-AI project. The focus is on human relational psychology, methodological possibilities, claim boundaries, and testable research questions.

## Public-release rules

1. Publish conceptual claims, references, and intentionally released evidence only.
2. Keep private conversation shorthand, romantic/relationship metaphors, identifiable third-party material, and client/confidential content out of public artifacts by default.
3. Separate observation, hypothesis, interpretation, and validated finding.
4. Do not infer AI consciousness, personhood, subjective attachment, or clinical efficacy from trajectory behavior.
5. Preserve version history and provenance when a claim changes.
